Our Story

A park the community saved

Palma Sola Botanical Park sits in the "Old Palma Sola" section of northwest Bradenton — a stretch of land with deep history in Manatee County and the Tampa Bay area, host to many cultures from the time of the Timucuan people through the Spanish era to today.

The park began in 1990, when local citizens and community groups rallied to save this 10-acre site — once the Manatee County Nursery — from being sold as surplus land. The Bradenton Herald helped build public support, and in 1993 the Palma Sola Botanical Park Foundation, Inc. was incorporated as a 501(c)(3) nonprofit to preserve the former nursery as green space. The park formally opened in partnership with Manatee County, and today remains a joint venture: the county owns the land and handles some infrastructure, while the Foundation runs the programming, collections, events, and volunteers that make the park worth visiting.

Two buildings tell part of the story. The Galleria, added after 2000, hosts weddings, parties, and classes. The Baden building — now the office and Board/Bride's Room — was originally a florist shop moved onto the property.

The park's secret is its climate: a protected, warm micro-climate that stays notably frost-free. Those unusual conditions let us showcase rare palms, fruits, flowering trees, and other subtropical plants that wouldn't survive elsewhere this far north. In 2008, the Manatee Rare Fruit Council planted — and still maintains — an extensive exotic fruit tree section.

Our Mission

A community resource for nature

To serve as a unique community resource dedicated to providing an interactive learning environment that fosters an appreciation of nature and landscaping, preserves our natural resources, and promotes environmental education.

📚

Promote environmental education

Classes, tours, and programs that connect people of every age to the living world around them.

🌿

Foster appreciation of nature

Ten acres of rare and beautiful plants, free to wander and discover any day of the year.

💧

Preserve natural resources

Protecting this green space, its collections, and the habitat it provides for local wildlife.
