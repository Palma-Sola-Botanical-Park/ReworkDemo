About the Foundation

Who we are

In 1990, local citizens rallied to save a 10-acre site that had served as the Manatee County Nursery. In 1993, the nonprofit 501(c)(3) Palma Sola Botanical Park Foundation, Inc. was established. In 1996, the park formally opened in partnership with Manatee County Government.

Today it's a joint venture between Manatee County parks department and the nonprofit Foundation. The county owns the land and handles some infrastructure. The Foundation handles programming, collections, events, volunteers, and everything that makes the park worth visiting.

We've been recognized by Bay News 9 ("Florida on a Tankful"), featured in national Horticulture magazine, and named Bradenton's Best. We're proud of all of that. We're more proud of the park being free and open 365 days a year.
