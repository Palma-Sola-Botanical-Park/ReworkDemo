Why It Matters

Your donation at work

Palma Sola Botanical Park receives zero federal, state, or local government subsidies. Every dollar that comes through the gate — or through a donation — goes directly into maintaining and growing 10 acres of subtropical botanical park, free to the public every single day of the year.

Recent projects funded by donations and grants: new composite deck and ramp (Bishop Parker Foundation), venue furniture (Selby Foundation), native plant restoration plantings, and ongoing pond-edge habitat work.
