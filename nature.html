iNaturalist Partnership

We rely on the community for identification

We're not a team of scientists. We're a park run by volunteers and community members who care about this place. iNaturalist gives us the infrastructure to document everything — and a global network of experts to help identify it. When we're not sure what something is, we post it and let the community weigh in. You can too.

The project is called Palma Sola Botanical Park Collection Project. It currently has 839 observations, 347 species, and 7 members. Every single one of those was submitted by a person walking through the park with their phone.

Why it matters

  • Every observation is a permanent, timestamped, geolocated scientific record
  • Bloom logs help track seasonal patterns over years — which plants flower in March vs October
  • Wildlife observations show which animals use which plants, and when
  • The data belongs to everyone — researchers, educators, future park visitors
  • You can disagree with an identification and the community will sort it out

What to observe

  • 🌸 A plant in bloom — log it and it becomes part of the seasonal record
  • 🦜 Any bird you can photograph — even blurry counts
  • 🦎 Lizards, frogs, and snakes on or near the path
  • 🦋 Butterflies landing on specific plants (the plant–insect relationship matters)
  • 🍄 Fungi — we have very few observations of fungi; this is a gap worth filling
  • 🐛 Caterpillars on host plants — links the plant collection to the wildlife record
