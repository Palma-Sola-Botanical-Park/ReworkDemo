How we know what's here

We're not scientists.
Neither are most of our observers.

iNaturalist is our field notebook.

We're a park run by volunteers and community members who care about this place. iNaturalist gives us the infrastructure to document everything — and a global network of experts to help identify it. When we're not sure what something is, we post it and let the community weigh in. You can too.

The project is called Palma Sola Botanical Park Collection Project. Every observation in it was submitted by a person walking through the park with their phone.

Why it matters

  • Every observation is a permanent, timestamped, geolocated scientific record
  • Bloom logs track seasonal patterns over years — which plants flower when
  • Wildlife observations show which animals use which plants, and when
  • The data belongs to everyone — researchers, educators, future visitors

What to photograph here

  • 🌸 A plant in bloom — log it and it becomes part of the seasonal record
  • 🦜 Any bird you can photograph — even blurry counts
  • 🦎 Lizards, frogs, and snakes on or near the path
  • 🦋 Butterflies landing on specific plants (the plant–insect relationship matters)
  • 🍄 Fungi — very few observations so far; a real gap worth filling
