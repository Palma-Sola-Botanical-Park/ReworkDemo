iNaturalist turns any photo into a scientific record. Here's how to add your observations to the Palma Sola Botanical Park project in about two minutes.

How it works

Five steps, once

After this setup you just photograph and submit — no extra steps.

1

Download the free app

iNaturalist is free on iOS and Android. No subscription, no ads. Made by the California Academy of Sciences and National Geographic.

2

Create a free account

Takes about 60 seconds. You'll need an email address. Your username can be anything — many people use their first name.

3

Allow location access

This is the key step. When location is on, iNaturalist automatically knows your photo was taken at Palma Sola — no manual entry needed.

4

Photograph anything

Plants, birds, insects, lizards, fungi — anything alive. Even blurry photos count. The AI suggests an ID and the community confirms it.

5

Add to our project

Search for "Palma Sola Botanical Park" in Projects and join. Then your observations automatically appear in our record.

Tips for the park

A few things worth knowing

Mark cultivated plants correctly. Most plants in the collection were intentionally planted here. When submitting, tap "Is it wild?" and select No — it's captive or cultivated. This keeps our data honest.

Wildlife observations don't need that tag. Birds, lizards, butterflies, and insects that just happen to be here are wild. Submit those normally.

Take multiple photos. A close-up of the leaf, a shot of the whole plant, and the flower if there is one. More photos = faster, more accurate identification by the community.

You don't need to know what it is. Submit anyway. The iNaturalist AI will make a suggestion and other users will confirm or correct it — sometimes within minutes.
