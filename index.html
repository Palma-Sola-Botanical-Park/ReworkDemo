Nature is here.
Come find it.

Ten acres at the edge of Palma Sola Bay — free, open, and genuinely yours.

This is a place to walk slowly, notice things, and come back again. To bring your dog, your kids, your camera, or just yourself. To volunteer on a Saturday morning and stay for lunch. To hear live music under the palms, celebrate a birthday in the gazebo, or sit quietly by the pond and watch what happens.

The plants here have names and stories. The birds have been coming for decades. The people who tend this place know it like a neighborhood — because it is one.

You don't have to be a botanist or a birder. You just have to show up.

The park, plainly

We're not Selby Gardens.
We're yours.

Selby has a team of scientists and a budget to match. We have 10 acres, a lot of volunteers, and a community that cares. That's actually enough to do something real.

Our plant collection has over 800 species. Through our iNaturalist project, community members have documented plants, birds, reptiles, insects, and fungi — and the record keeps growing. Every observation was submitted by a person walking through the park with their phone.

Community Science

Every observation counts

We track bloom seasons by logging when things flower. We document which birds show up and when. We note what's fruiting, what's emerging, what's in decline. None of this works without people walking through the park with their phones out.
